Revolutionizing Liver Care

Predicting Liver Cirrhosis using Advanced Machine Learning

Enter a patient's clinical details and get an instant, model-based prediction of the liver disease stage (1–4), or run a quick cirrhosis risk check from alcohol and blood-test history.

Check Liver Stage Risk Check

How it works

  1. Enter details — the 16 clinical values (drug, age, blood tests) or the 16 risk-check values.
  2. Model prediction — a trained scikit-learn model predicts the liver stage (1 = normal, 2 = fatty liver, 3 = fibrosis, 4 = cirrhosis) or a YES/no cirrhosis risk.
  3. Receive results — the result, the model's confidence, and a downloadable/printable report.

Built for doctors and healthcare professionals (see the Empathy Map and Customer Journey Map in the original project). Every prediction is logged and can be reviewed on the History page.

Models: five classifiers were trained and compared on the Mayo Clinic PBC data (412 patients) and on the Kaggle Indian liver dataset (896 patients). Real measured metrics are on the Models page — the original report's performance table was blank, so no numbers here are invented.
